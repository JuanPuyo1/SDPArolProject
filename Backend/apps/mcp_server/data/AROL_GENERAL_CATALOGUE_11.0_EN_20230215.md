## WHAT WE DO

We design, produce and globally deliver capping/
closure systems. We provide worldwide leading 
brands in several business areas:

Food
Beverage
Wine & spirit
Household care
Personal care
Pharmaceutical
Chemical

## OUR MISSION

Our mission is being globally recognized as 
a top of mind brand specialized in safety of 
closing solutions.
AROL was founded in 1978. In keeping with 
its mission has steadily grown and globally 
succeeded in being recognized as a capping 
and closing solutions specialist.

## OUR VALUES

We decided to always get the challenge. We 
fulfill the commitments trying always to build a 
long term relationships.

## OUR STRATEGY

It is based on few but reliable pillars:
a real attitude for top quality products.
We cross-pollinate best practices from multiple 
industries to internally spread our design know-
how: we believe this can persistently empower 
our open mind attitude to innovate.
